## 1. Phase 5 (branch `phase-5`)

Decisions under **2026-09-24 — Phase 5** in `docs/decisions.md`; the contract is `docs/interop.md`.

### Built, by brief item
1. **Export** (`app/src/export/`, the new **Files** tab): region pack, GeoJSON and TopoJSON of the

2. **Import** (`app/src/import/`): a RegionPack is validated and its mesh version checked; from another

   GeoJSON and KML become a split by majority overlap, a snap layer, or the scope.
3. **Save and load:** an IndexedDB library in the Files tab, every call wrapped; file upload and
   download; the share link only for splits a link can reproduce, with the reason when not.
4. **`docs/interop.md`:** where packs live, the RegionPack v1 contract, versioning rules, and the
   twenty-line Leaflet snippet.
5. **Tests**, below.

The three presets moved to the top-level `packs/` as `<slug>.v1.json` (unchanged bytes); a Vite plugin
serves them in dev and copies them into the build. The Region panel and the Markdown both render
`dossier/fields.ts`, so they share one order; the panel gained the distance-to-capital fact.

### Problems found on the way
- **Zero-area spikes in exported polygons.** geojsonhint flagged five canada-26 regions: the cell
  topology stores a few edges as two identical arcs (one per cell), which walk as A→B→C→B→A "holes".
  Arcs used twice by a region are now interior, and rings with no area are dropped; both presets now
  pass geojsonhint with no messages.
- **KML re-import lost multi-part regions.** @tmcw/togeojson reads a MultiGeometry of several polygons
  as a GeometryCollection, which the template reader ignored. It now takes polygons from any of the
  three shapes.
- **New Vitest files need the node environment** to read repo files (`new URL` under jsdom is not a
  file URL); they parse XML and HTML with @xmldom/xmldom.
- **The interop test's second hover** found two tooltips: a synthetic mouseout does not close
  Leaflet's first one. The test now looks for the region's own label.

## 2. Phase 6 (branch `phase-6`)

Decisions under **2026-09-24 — Phase 6: scenarios and game hooks** in `docs/decisions.md`; the plan's
Phase 6 section carries the amendment (two presets, one overlay, the House of Cards example).

### Built, by brief item
1. **Divergence mode** (`app/src/scenario/`, the new **Scenario** tab). A scenario replays the atlas's
   event list with its own YAML events merged in after the base's on the same date; base events whose
   `requires:` it breaks, or that act on a unit it removed, are skipped with a notice. The result is an
   atlas of the same shape, so the map, timeline, unit panel and atlas scopes need no changes. UI: a badge
   over the map (skipped count, back to the record), the scenario's ticks on the timeline in its colour,
   the diff against the base on the timeline's date (in the tab, and the record's removed or redrawn units
   dashed on the map), a "skipped in this scenario" notice in Details, and "resolves cleanly". A pack
   made in a scenario carries it in `meta.scenario`, and loading the pack restores it.
2. **Nesting** (`app/src/splitter/tree.ts`). "Split this region" in the Region tab; the child's scope is
   the region, and its pack records `meta.parentPack` and `meta.parentRegionId`. A breadcrumb over the
   Generate, Region, Set and Files tabs; the Files tab downloads the tree as one JSON (the root pack with
   `children`), and importing it restores every level.
3. **Overlays** (`app/src/overlays/`): point and flow kinds, drawn in their own pane over any partition.
   Immigrant-share halos over the 40 CMAs ship, in the layers menu's new Overlays group. The rotational
   workforce flows are in `docs/backlog.md`.
4. **Presets:** `docs/scenarios/newfoundland-independent-1949.yaml` and `docs/scenarios/buffalo-1905.yaml`,
   each with a premise paragraph and cited events, compiled by `npm run scenarios` (checked in CI).
5. **Scores:** `regions[].stats.score` (population, gdp, resource_index, cohesion, exposure), defined in
   `docs/interop.md` with `meridian.getScores(packUrl)` and the House of Cards worked example: the new
   `atlasSovereign` scope takes Canada as the atlas has it on 1 July 1867, the fourth preset
   `dominion-1867-5` splits it into five, and the page prints the scores the helper returns.

### Pipeline change
`requires:` on two events in `pipeline/atlas/events.yaml` (1949: Newfoundland under Britain; 2001:
under Canada), passed through by `atlas/build.py`, which fails if the base breaks its own precondition.
`atlas.v1.json` → `0edd7ad7…` (golden and `SHA256SUMS` updated); `atlas.v1.topojson.gz` unchanged.

### Problems found on the way
- **Pack ids hashed the spec's key order.** `packId` used `JSON.stringify(spec)`, and a spec read back
  from a pack has its keys in another order, so the same split got another id. It now hashes canonical
  JSON (sorted keys). Found by the nesting test.
- **The atlas layer cached drawings by unit and start date only.** A scenario row can share both with a
  base row and have another drawing; the cache key now includes the drawing, name and sovereign.
- **Atlas-scoped packs could race the atlas load:** a `#pack=dominion-1867-5` link resolves its scope
  from the atlas, which might not be loaded yet. Loading such a pack, or running such a spec, now waits
  for the atlas.
- **Smoke:** the first scenario tests matched the Scenario tab's own event buttons as well as the timeline
  ticks; they are scoped to the timeline now. The app was right.

## 3. Phase 7 (branch `phase-7`)

Decisions are under **2026-09-24 — Phase 7: hardening and 1.0** in `docs/decisions.md`. The
numbers are in `docs/perf.md` (Phase 7 section).

### Built, by brief item
1. **Performance.**
   - **Event crossings are a visibility swap.** The atlas keeps the three event windows either side
     of the current one attached and hidden, and refills them in idle slices budgeted by vertex
     count. The worst one-year step fell from 17.5–30.8 ms to 1.7–7.3 ms at 1867 and 1999; the test
     now asserts the maximum under 16 ms.
   - The "backlog item" named in the brief was not in `docs/backlog.md`. It was the p95 note in
     `docs/perf.md`.
   - **Splitter data decoded once and kept in IndexedDB,** keyed by the artefacts' fingerprinted
     URLs.
   - **The worker gets the mesh once;** template assignments are transferred.
   - **Scope graphs are cached by mask.**
   - Measured and recorded: frame times with a 30-region Canada split (every slider step under
     16 ms, 11.1 ms at worst), and the memory of the 38,432-cell split under iPad emulation. The JS
     heap stays flat at 117 MB, and the renderer levels off at about 690 MB.
   - Lazy-loading the layer TopoJSON and a canvas renderer for the split were already in place; a
     smoke test now holds the first.
2. **Offline.** A service worker written by the build:
   - it precaches every file in `dist`;
   - it keeps the basemap tiles of the last view, and drops the rest;
   - the page is network-first, with the cached page as fallback.

   The offline smoke test saves a pack, goes offline, reloads, opens the pack, and sees the last
   view's tiles.
3. **Accessibility.**
   - Timeline: Page Up and Page Down jump between events, and the ticks are one tab stop moved with
     the arrow keys.
   - Panel tabs follow the WAI-ARIA tabs pattern, with a focus ring everywhere.
   - A keyboard-only Generate run is tested, with focus in panel order.
   - axe finds no WCAG 2.1 A/AA violations in any tab.
   - The region palette was rechosen for deuteranopia. Its worst pair went from 3.8 to 12.8
     CIEDE2000 as drawn, and all 28 pairs are checked in four conditions.
4. **Content.**
   - *No 1912 extensions* and *Maritime Union*, cited, with tests. Written by a subagent in a
     worktree and cherry-picked as `74740dd`.
   - The dominion-1867-5 Labrador sentence.
5. **Docs.**
   - The data refresh playbook in `pipeline/README.md`, with a table of what breaks for old packs
     and links.
   - `CHANGELOG.md` from the tags.
   - A Licences and sources dialog generated from `docs/data-sources.md`: 10 attribution strings
     covering 53 sources, with a CI check.
   - The README front page, drafted for Mark to edit, with the honest limits verbatim.

### Problems found on the way
- **The first lookahead attached rows whole inside idle slices,** and one row (the NWT of 1880, 45k
  vertices) took 12–17 ms. Splitting rows into one path per part to slice them made crossings
  slower, so it was reverted; slices are now budgeted by vertex count instead.
- **Chromium declared no idle period for over a minute** after a pan with a split on the map, and the
  refill stalled. Its idle request now has a timeout.
- **The service worker's cache missed the page's own script and stylesheet.** The install step
  fetched them with other headers than a module script sends; lookups now ignore `Vary`.
- **The tile cache kept the previous zoom level's tiles.** Leaflet keeps them in the pane for a
  moment after `load`, so only the current zoom's tiles are reported.
- **Reading `indexedDB` throws where site data is blocked,** and the new cache did not catch that.
  The existing blocked-storage smoke test found it.
- **Atlas smoke counted hidden paths** once the lookahead kept rows attached. It now counts shown
  paths.
- **On iPad the sheet covered the map's attribution line,** OSM and CARTO credits included. It is at
  the top right on narrow screens now.
- **The region palette failed its own check by a wide margin** (3.8), and so did the published
  colour-blind-safe palettes (about 5.5) at the old 0.42 fill opacity.

### Not done, and why
- **No 1912 extensions stops holding in 1927 and 1999.** Holding it to today needs a `minus`

- **About 800 ms of main-thread work when a 30-region split lands** (the dossiers). Backlog.
- **The manual iPad pass** (plan Phase 7, "You do": Safari, offline after first load, memory) is

## 4. Release 1.0.1 (branch `release-1.0.1`)

Decisions are under **2026-09-24 — Release 1.0.1** in `docs/decisions.md`; the numbers are in
`docs/perf.md`, "Release 1.0.1".

### Built, by brief item
1. **The worker lands the split.**
   - It loads its own splitter data and cell topology.
   - For a run, it does prepare, solve, finish, names, colours, dossiers, set analysis, scores, rings
     and node id; the page sends only the spec and the scope mask.
   - Splits made on the page are described in the worker too.
   - The page's remaining work is split across separate tasks, and a fit that changes nothing is
     skipped.
   - The longest main-thread task while a 30-region Canada split lands was 768–824 ms; now none
     reaches 50 ms on either viewport, and `split.spec.ts` asserts it.
2. **acadie-2.**
   - The recipe: NB + NS + PE as one scope (the new `provinces` scope kind), bisected on
     `french_share`, N = 2, hard contiguity, refined.
   - Acadie: 282,549 people, 66% French mother tongue, one piece. The Maritimes: 1,609,127.
   - The names come from the recipe's `nameBy` rule; the premise is in `meta.premise`.
   - "Acadian Maritimes" is gone from the scenario lists in the vision and the plan, and points at
     the preset. The Maritime Union scenario stays, renamed `maritime-union`.
3. **Backlog:** the 1.1 headline, "scenario overlays may use the same boundary primitives as base
   events", with No 1912 extensions to today as its first test case.
4. **iPad findings from Mark:** none, so nothing to fix.

### Problems found on the way
- **The first worker landing still had one 138 ms task:** React's commit, the ring decode, the fit
  and the draw together. They were split into separate tasks. The fit alone was 41–53 ms on the
  iPad layout, because it re-projects every path on the map; it is skipped when the split is already
  in view.
- **The page's cache write landed mid-run** (648 ms): an IndexedDB put serializes about 17 MB
  synchronously. Only the worker writes the cache now.
- **Long-frame entries arrive after their frame ends,** so the page's own data decode before the
  click was counted against the landing. The perf spec now counts only entries that start after the
  click.
- **The first Generate open fetched the five artefacts twice,** page and worker in parallel. Found
  by the loading smoke test. The worker now loads after the page, from cache.
- **Pack loads showed solver names for a moment,** because describing became asynchronous. Found by
  the files smoke test. Packs now show their own names at once.
- **Renderer memory kept rising about 9 MB per run.** With GC forced in the page and the worker it is
  flat from run 7, at about 870 MB under iPad emulation. That is the worker's own copy of the data,
  not a leak; sharing it is in the backlog.
- **The acadie-2 premise was checked against the pack.** One Évangéline cell (Wellington) joins
  Acadie by a sea crossing, and mother tongue is not home language; both are corrected in the text.
