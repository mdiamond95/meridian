# 2026-10-08 — Release 1.0.4: a hex unit table for consumers (branch `hex-table`)

Decisions are under **2026-10-08 — Release 1.0.4** in `docs/decisions.md`. `pre-1.0.4` was tagged on
`eba1caf` before any change.

## Before building
- Read `docs/interop.md` (unit tables, consumer rules, versioning rules), the 1.0.2 and 1.0.3
  decisions, `pipeline/README.md` and `pipeline/ridings.py`.
- **CSD type:** the boundary file carries `CSDTYPE` (56 codes), so the stop condition did not apply.
  The labels come from Statistics Canada's tables (92-500-G 2020 Table 4.4, plus GR, TAL and TWL from
  92-500-G 2025 Table 4.2).
- **Lakes:** the cartographic CSDs leave out the sea, Hudson Bay, the Great Lakes and Lake of the
  Woods, and include Lake Winnipeg, Great Bear, Great Slave and the other large inland lakes. Land
  follows the CSDs, "as ridings.v1 measures it".
- **Pages:** both 1.0.3 deploy jobs (runs 37362807026, 37365421408) were cancelled by GitHub after
  about 15 minutes queued, before any step ran. Pages has not deployed since `db8fd19`. Not touched
  here.

## Built, by brief item
1. **`pipeline/hexes.py`** writes `data/build/hexes.r4.v1.json.gz` (407,618 bytes): format
   `meridian.unitTable`, version 1, unit `h3_r4`, 6,011 rows in H3 index order, with every field in the
   brief.
   - The values are aggregated from the mesh cells as a pack region aggregates them.
   - Land, province, neighbours and jurisdictions come from the CSDs and the atlas.
   - The code shared with ridings moved to `pipeline/unittables.py`. `ridings.v1.json.gz` was rebuilt
     after the move and is byte-identical.
2. **`data/build/layers/hexes.r4.v1.topojson.gz`** (648,414 bytes): each row's land as one geometry,
   in row order, with shared arcs. The 21 open-water rows are null geometries.
3. **The contract:**
   - The Zod schema is now a union on `unit`, with `fed_2023` unchanged and `h3_r4` new, exported to
     `docs/schemas/unitTable.schema.json`.
   - Both files are declared in `artefacts.yaml`, recorded in `SHA256SUMS`, and part of
     `make validate`, which mirrors the invariants, `make build` and `make verify`.
   - `packs:check` holds every released unit table immutable, and the layer it names in `meta.layer`.
4. **`docs/interop.md`:** `h3_r4` in the unit-table section, covering the fields, the neighbour rule,
   stable H3 ids and the clipped layer, plus a note that the union widens the 1.0.3 schema. CHANGELOG,
   decisions, this log and the pipeline README are updated. The rendered layer is
   `docs/img/hexes.r4.v1.png`.

### Neighbours across water
By the rule as stated (a link is land when any of the common edge lies on land), Newfoundland is linked
to Labrador by land over the Strait of Belle Isle. Prince Edward Island has 7 land and 9 water links to
the mainland. Vancouver Island reaches the mainland by land through the Gulf Islands. A 45 km hexagon
holds both shores of a narrower strait. Not tuned; `docs/interop.md` says so, and the decisions entry
offers a rule that would look inside the hexagons.
