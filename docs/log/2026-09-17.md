## C. Sitting B — UI, constraints and presets

Branch `phase-3b`. Decisions are under **2026-09-17 — Phase 3 Sitting B** in `docs/decisions.md`.

### Built, by brief item
1. **Generate panel** (a tab in the right panel): scope (Canada, province, atlas unit at the current
   date, a region of the current split, a drawn polygon), method, N, seed, lens presets (Economic,
   Demographic, Linguistic, Indigenous-constitutional, Physical, Political, Internal-colony index)
   with per-column sliders and "add a column", balance target, contiguity, compactness weight, run and
   cancel with progress from the worker.
2. **Constraints:** min and max population per region; keep-together and keep-apart pins, picked by
   clicking the map or searching the gazetteer; carve CMAs above a threshold first; snap layers
   (rivers, basins, Continental Divide, treaties, CD and CSD edges, 0.5° graticule, ridings, ecozones)
   with a snap weight. Township lines are listed as unavailable: no DLS data yet.
3. **Rendering:** regions dissolved from the hex topology in the app, eight hues assigned so
   neighbours differ, region names on the map, hover stats, legend with per-region details; the map
   fits a new split's scope.
4. **Manual override:** select a region, paint cells; stats and names recompute; `edited` and the edit
   log go into the downloaded pack.
5. **URL state:** `#split=` reruns a spec, `#pack=` loads a preset; followed on load and on hashchange.
6. **Presets** (amended; no hand-drawn templates): alberta-15, canada-26 and canada-14, generated by
   the engine into `app/public/packs/` with their full spec; `npm run presets -w app` regenerates them.

### Problems found on the way, and what was done
- **The capitals preset was wrong twice.** Balanced growth let the St. John's region reach Laval; then,
  with no balance target, annealing optimised compactness alone and Edmonton's region lost Edmonton.
  The preset is now growth only: the land nearest each capital.
- **Carving Toronto missed 1.2 million people:** cell centres in Lake Ontario. CMA membership now
  goes through each cell's CSD.
- **Region names:** naming by the most populous place's point misnamed regions holding most of a split
  city ("Clearwater County", 616k people). Names now come from the CSD with the most people inside the
  region, and are unique.
- **A share link pasted into an open tab did nothing:** only the initial hash was read. The app now
  listens for hashchange.

### Memory on the Codespace
- The VS Code Playwright extension's `test-server` was running alongside `make verify`; it was stopped
  (available memory 2,994 → 3,478 MB) and kept off by a watcher until verify exited. The watcher's
  first version matched its own command line and stopped itself; the second, a script file, did not.
- Smoke now runs with `workers: 1`; the determinism test launches one engine at a time. The serial
  order is in `docs/perf.md`. Nothing was killed.
