## Deviations (see decisions.md)

- TypeScript 6.0: typescript-eslint does not support 7 yet.
- CARTO tiles need a free key, otherwise they are watermarked; `CARTO_BASEMAPS_KEY` repo variable → `VITE_CARTO_KEY`.
